What does this mean for your equity?

Home equity = Current market value − Outstanding mortgage debt and other liens.

Appreciation can increase home equity if the outstanding debt stays the same or decreases. Mortgage principal repayment can also build equity, even if your home's market value doesn't rise.

Example: If a home is worth $200,000 and the mortgage balance is $160,000, equity is $40,000. If the home value rises to $206,000 while the mortgage balance stays at $160,000, equity increases to $46,000.

Appreciation is not guaranteed, and equity is not the same as cash available after selling. Mortgage debt, other liens, transaction costs, maintenance, taxes, and inflation all matter when evaluating wealth.
